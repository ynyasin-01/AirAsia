AIRASIA · TRAVEL DEMO

About this project

A simpler way to explore your next journey.

Explore. Plan. Try it out.

AirAsia is an independent educational project inspired by modern travel websites. Explore sample flights, compare stays and try the booking experience from search to confirmation.

A clear demo experience

Flights, hotels, availability and fares are illustrative. No real tickets, payments, confirmation emails or hotel reservations are issued. This project is not affiliated with AirAsia.

Your reservations

Demo bookings are stored in this browser on this website. View all your saved flights and hotels, download confirmations or cancel reservations on the Bookings page. Clearing browser storage removes saved reservations.

How booking works

  1. 01

    Search

    Pick your route and dates. Fares update instantly in BDT or any currency you choose.

  2. 02

    Compare

    See baggage, transit time and total price side by side — no hidden fees added later.

  3. 03

    Reserve

    Review the total and save a demo reservation. No payment is collected and no real ticket is issued.

Why Book with AirAsia

A simple flight booking demonstration, from search to confirmation.

Easy Search

Choose your route, travel dates and passengers. Compare sample departures and book in a few steps.

Clear Pricing

View fares in your preferred currency, with sample taxes included in deals and optional baggage shown at checkout.

Manage Bookings

View saved flights and hotels in separate booking lists. Download your itinerary or cancel a demo booking in this browser.
